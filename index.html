03 / Teaching

Helping people learn by doing.

I support peers and learners by making technical concepts simpler, more relatable, and easier to apply. My teaching style focuses on clarity, practice, and real-world relevance rather than memorization.

I believe the best learning happens when a concept is explained clearly, demonstrated simply, and then practiced hands-on. That approach builds both confidence and understanding.

I am especially interested in the future role of AI, automation, and practical digital literacy in education.
